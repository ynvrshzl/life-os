The architecture of life-os is, as following all of our [cyber-ethos](https://github.com/ynvrshzl/cyberethos) projects: plain-text files and folders on the local system.

The entire system is defined in plain-text files + folders, local to the human user's system. These are portable across devices, and local first.

- lifeos/                 
  - workspaces/
    - coding.json
    - office.json
    - art-study.json
  - app/
    - config.json
